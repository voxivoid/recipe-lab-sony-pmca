# Video compatibility

`Long recording` removes the ordinary 29:50 product limit on every body where Sony's three recording-limit slots are
present. The RX100 IV and RX100 V also have a separate five-minute 4K/120p limit; Recipe Lab changes its slot only on
those two model codes. It never changes the camera's temperature shutdown.

| Recipe Lab body | Internal 4K | Stock continuous limit relevant here |
|---|---:|---|
| A6300, A6500 | yes | 29 min; heat can stop 4K earlier |
| A7R II, A7S II, A99 II | yes | 29 min; heat can stop 4K earlier |
| RX10 II, RX10 III | yes | 29 min; heat can stop 4K earlier |
| RX100 IV, RX100 V | yes | 5 min in 4K and HD 120p/100p; 29 min otherwise |
| A7S | HDMI output only | 29 min for internal HD |
| A6000, A5100, A5000, A7, A7 II, A7R, RX1R II | no | 29 min for internal HD |
| NEX-5R, NEX-5T, NEX-6, A68, A77 II | no | 29 min for internal HD |
| RX100 II, RX100 III, original RX10, HX60, HX90, HX400, WX500 | no | 29 min for internal HD; some MP4 modes stop earlier at their file-size limit |

This change only edits the camera's recording-limit setting. It does not add start/stop recording to Recipe Lab.
To record a look, keep Picture Profile off, store the recipe with centre (or Apply edited values), restart the camera,
leave Recipe Lab, and record in the camera's normal Movie mode. Creative Style and white-balance based looks carry into
that mode; recipes with the `JPEG only` badge use Picture Effect and are not video recipes.

The row compiles for every app-capable body, but the settings-store IDs still need a report from each model. A6300 is
the first target. RX100 IV/V especially need confirmation that the separate 4K slot is writable and takes effect on
their installed firmware.
