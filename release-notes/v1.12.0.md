New
- Share a custom recipe: hold MENU on it in the recipe list and pick Share. Pick a shape (4:5 post, 1:1 square or 9:16 story) and Save image puts a picture of the recipe, with its settings and a QR code, in the camera's playback like any photo. Show QR code puts the code on the screen for someone next to you to scan.
- Read Recipe QR Code (hold MENU): point the camera at a recipe's QR code, from Recipe Lab Studio or another camera, and press the shutter. The recipe is added to your custom recipes. The photo used to read the code is deleted when you leave Recipe Lab.
- The links of your custom recipes are in RECIPES/LINKS.TXT on the memory card. Copy one into a chat or a post: opened on a phone with Recipe Lab Studio, it adds the recipe; anywhere else it shows the recipe on recipelab.io.

Changed
- The recipe picture has a new look, with the Inter, Archivo and Space Mono typefaces, and playback files it under the day it was saved.
- Custom recipes are now saved in a newer file format that names the cameras they are for. Recipe Lab 1.11.1 and older skip these files, so update Recipe Lab on every camera you move a card between. Recipe files saved by older versions still load.
- Recipe Lab Studio sends recipes only to cameras with this version: update Recipe Lab before Sync recipes.

Please try
- Share a recipe, Save image in each shape, and open it in playback: the picture should show like a photo, and its QR code should scan with a phone. Then Show QR code and read it with Read Recipe QR Code on a second camera, or point a phone at it.
- Read Recipe QR Code from a QR on a phone screen: the recipe should be added and selected, and the photo should be gone from the card once you leave Recipe Lab.
- Save a new custom recipe, then look at its file in the RECIPES folder of the card on a computer: it should start with "format: 2" and "family: sony-creative-style". Pick it again after a power cycle: the look should stick.
- Save, rename and delete custom recipes, then open RECIPES/LINKS.TXT from the card on a computer: it should list each recipe with its link, and opening a link on a phone should show that recipe.
- Put a card with recipes saved by an older Recipe Lab in the camera: they should all still show under Custom.
