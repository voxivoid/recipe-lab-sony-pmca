New
- Custom recipes: change a recipe and press Save as new, or hold MENU → New recipe to keep the camera's current settings. Name it with the on-screen keyboard. It is saved on the memory card and listed under Custom in the brand list.
- Share recipes by copying the .YML files in the card's RECIPES folder over USB.
- An edited recipe shows Save, Apply, Save as new and Discard under its settings. Apply stores your edits without saving a new recipe.
- The app asks before throwing away edits you have not saved or applied.
- An EDITED badge shows when the values are not the recipe's own.
- In the brand list, hold MENU on a custom recipe to rename or delete it.
- The factory look is back in the list, first under Sony, and can be a favourite.
- The panel can hide just the key hints ("No keys").
