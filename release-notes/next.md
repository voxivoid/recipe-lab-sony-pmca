New
- If a Picture Profile is on when you open the app, it asks to turn it off. Recipes have no effect while one is on, so choose Turn off. Keep on leaves it as it was.

Please try
- On a camera with a Picture Profile menu (A6300, A6500, A7 II, A7S II and others), turn a Picture Profile on, open the app and choose Turn off. Check whether the preview in the app changes, then leave the app and check MENU → Picture Profile says Off, also after a power cycle. Report it on GitHub.
