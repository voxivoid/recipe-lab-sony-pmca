New
- Half-press the shutter on the live view and a box shows where the camera is focusing: amber dashed while it focuses, green when focus locks, an amber double line when focus is uncertain. Let go and it goes. It is never in the photo, and with a manual lens or MF there is simply no box.
- Hold MENU → Display: one place for what sits over the live image. Panel visibility moved here, and beside it you can turn on a live histogram (top right) and a rule-of-thirds grid, or hide the recipe counter. Restore defaults puts these back to how the app started.
- The app now opens with the panel as you left it (Full, No keys, Label or Hidden) instead of always on Full.
- Hold MENU → Open with a key: put Recipe Lab on a button. Turn it on, then in the camera's Custom Key Settings set a key (C1, say) to Download Appli. That key then opens Recipe Lab from the shooting screen, also after switching the camera off and on. The camera keeps calling it Download Appli.; that name cannot be changed by an app. Tried on an A6000.

- On a camera other than the A6000: turn Open with a key on and check the set key opens Recipe Lab. If the message says the camera did not take it, hold MENU → Save a bug report and attach RECIPLAB/REPORT.TXT to a GitHub issue.
