Fixed
- Transfer to phone on the A6300 said "This camera has no Wi-Fi Direct mode" and never turned the Wi-Fi on. It now starts the camera's Wi-Fi network and the phone can join it.

Please try
- On another camera where Transfer to phone said "This camera has no Wi-Fi Direct mode" (for example the A6500, RX100 V or RX10 III): hold MENU → Transfer to phone again and check it says Ready and the phone can join its Wi-Fi. If it says Error, hold MENU → Save a bug report and attach the file to a GitHub issue: it records each step of starting the Wi-Fi.
