New
- The wheel now loops only through the list you picked your recipe from in the brand list: pick from Favorites and the wheel turns through your favorites only, pick from a brand and it stays in that brand. Recipe Lab remembers it the next time you open the app. A new All list at the top of the brand list brings back every recipe.
- Hold MENU → Open with a key: put Recipe Lab on a button. Turn it on, then in the camera's Custom Key Settings set a key (C1, say) to Download Appli. and select the app there. That key then opens Recipe Lab from the shooting screen, also after switching the camera off and on. The camera keeps calling it Download Appli.; that name cannot be changed by an app. Tried on an A6000.

Changed
- The brand list opens on the list you last picked from, with All, Favorites and Custom at the top.
- The panel's recipe count includes your custom recipes: they come after the built-in ones, as the wheel walks them.

Please try
- On a camera other than the A6000: turn Open with a key on and check the set key opens Recipe Lab. If the message says the camera did not take it, hold MENU → Save a bug report and attach RECIPLAB/REPORT.TXT to a GitHub issue.
- Turn Open with a key off and press the key again: it should open Sony's app store as before. (Removing Recipe Lab is fine either way: the key then shows the camera's own "application is either not set or has been uninstalled" message.)
