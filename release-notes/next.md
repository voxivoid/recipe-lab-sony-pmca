New
- Meet Recipe Lab Studio, the phone app for your Recipe Lab looks: it develops your RAWs with these recipes plus film grain and mist, on iPhone, iPad and Android (https://recipelab.io/studio).
- Hold MENU → Transfer to phone: the camera makes its own Wi-Fi network and shows its name and password. Join it from the phone and Studio pulls your RAWs and JPEGs straight off the card. No cable, no router, nothing on the card is changed.
- Hold MENU → Sync recipes: your custom recipes go to Studio and the ones made in Studio come back to the card, ready under Custom. Studio can also delete a custom recipe from the card.

Please try
- On a camera other than the A6000, hold MENU → Transfer to phone and check it says Ready and the phone can join its Wi-Fi. If it says Error, report the line under the card on GitHub.
