Added
- Picture profiles, for the A6000 on firmware 3.21: a new Pic Profiles group with S-Log2, S-Log3 (S-Gamut3.Cine or S-Gamut3), ITU709, HLG and ARRI LogC3. They are recipes: the GAMMA chip adds a profile to any recipe, and black level, black gamma, knee, colour saturation, colour phase and an S-curve shape it. Save your own like any custom recipe. A profile covers live view, photos and video, and comes back by itself after the camera is switched off and on, without opening the app (live view can show the normal look for up to a minute after switching on).

Changed
- The chips are grouped, with a line between the groups. A new LOOK chip switches between a Creative Style and a Picture Effect; the effect chip no longer has an "off" of its own.
- The tip line over the live view no longer shows on the No keys panel, only on the Full one.

Fixed
- A chip now grows to fit its value; a longer value, such as S-Log2 on the GAMMA chip, was cut short.
- The list of keys on the About page and in the key log names the control wheel and the top dial again; they showed as blank gaps.
