Added
- Picture profiles, for the A6000 on firmware 3.21: a new Pic Profiles group with S-Log2, S-Log3 (S-Gamut3.Cine or S-Gamut3), ITU709, HLG and ARRI LogC3. They are recipes: the GAMMA chip adds a profile to any recipe, and black level, black gamma, knee, colour saturation, colour phase and an S-curve shape it. Save your own like any custom recipe. A profile covers live view, photos and video, stays until the camera is switched off, and comes back when you open the app.
